Welcome to DIU Alumni Association.

Dear Alumni,

Welcome to the official website of the Daffodil 
International University Alumni Association (DIUAA).
 
As the Convenor of this organization, I am thrilled to 
connect with you all.

We are a vibrant network of graduates from Bangladesh's 
leading private university. DIUAA is dedicated to fostering
lifelong bonds, celebrating our achievements, and contributing
to our alma mater's growth. Our mission is rooted in unity,
success, and community impact, bringing together diverse talents
for collaboration and support.

Thank you for your dedication and passion. Together,
 we can inspire, connect, and thrive, building a future 
 that honors our proud past.

Warm regards,

Professor Dr. M. R. Kabir
Convenor, DIUAA.
